## Selected projects

### [ParliamentAI — GraphRAG for UK Parliamentary Debates](https://github.com/RonitBulani/graphrag-uk-parliament)

A local, full-stack research prototype built around graph-guided and hybrid retrieval over UK parliamentary Brexit speeches. The original experiment processed **39,423 speeches** into **201,131 text chunks** and evaluated retrieval systems on **50 multi-hop questions**. I extended the work into a **FastAPI + React** interface with evidence-linked answers, source inspection, saved conversations and exports, with **Docker** and **GitHub Actions** for local delivery and checks.

[View v1.0.0 release](https://github.com/RonitBulani/graphrag-uk-parliament/releases/tag/v1.0.0) · [Architecture](https://github.com/RonitBulani/graphrag-uk-parliament/blob/main/docs/ARCHITECTURE.md) · [Research results](https://github.com/RonitBulani/graphrag-uk-parliament/blob/main/results/RESULTS.md)

### [Byzantine-Resilient Communication for Multi-Agent RL](https://github.com/RonitBulani/byzantine-resilient-marl-comms)

My **individual contribution to a five-person MSc project**: designed and implemented the communication subsystem, including four Byzantine attack behaviours and broadcast, gossip, trimmed-mean and reputation-based communication protocols. This repository is a standalone extract of my contribution, not the full team training system.

### [Amazon Product Analytics — PostgreSQL & Clustering](https://github.com/RonitBulani/amazon-product-clustering-postgres)

A **scoped extract of my contribution to a university group project** covering exploratory data analysis, PostgreSQL tables and indexing, and unsupervised product clustering. Includes comparisons of K-Means, Bisecting K-Means and Gaussian Mixture Models, with documented evaluation caveats.

## Industry dissertation

I worked on a Network Rail drainage asset modelling project, developing a Python workflow to estimate degradation matrices from inspection histories. The work included continuous-time Markov modelling, validation against simulated data, temporal holdout checks, diagnostics and additional asset-level analysis. The industry dataset and code are not published here.
